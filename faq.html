Still Have Questions? ↗
What services does Pearl Softech provide?

We focus on three areas: software and web development, digital marketing, and custom business management systems such as CRM, ERP, HR and project platforms.

Can you build software specifically for our business?

Yes. We design and develop custom web applications, SaaS products, dashboards, portals, automation systems and integrations around your specific workflows.

Do you work with startups and established companies?

Yes. The delivery model ranges from MVP development and launch support to larger internal systems, integrations, and digital modernization programs.

How long will our project take?

It depends on scope, complexity, integrations, and feedback cycles. After discovery, we provide a realistic delivery plan rather than guessing from a generic package.

Can you improve an existing website or system?

Yes. We can assess whether the best move is optimization, redesign, modernization, integration, or a full rebuild.

Do you handle both design and development?

Yes. Design, front-end, back-end, integrations, deployment and analytics are all handled under one roof.

How do you price projects?

Most engagements are fixed-scope or retainer-based. After a discovery call, we provide a written estimate with milestones and deliverables in NPR or USD.

Do you offer ongoing support after launch?

Yes. We offer support, maintenance, and improvement retainers for clients who want us to stay involved after the initial launch.

How do we get started?

Book a consultation or submit the project form. We'll discuss the business objective, current situation, requirements, and the appropriate next step.
