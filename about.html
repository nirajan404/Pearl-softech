Home/About

Technology should make businesses more capable — not more complicated.

Pearl Softech brings software development, digital marketing, and business automation together under one strategy.

Our storyOne partner across product, growth and operations.

Most businesses don't have one technology problem. They have a chain of them — the website that doesn't convert, the marketing that isn't tracked, the ops tool that doesn't talk to the CRM, the spreadsheet that's become mission-critical.

We started Pearl Softech in Kathmandu to solve the chain. Instead of stitching together an agency for marketing, a freelancer for the website, and a consultancy for operations, businesses get one team that understands how all of it fits together.

Our goal is straightforward: turn complicated business challenges into technology that is useful, measurable, maintainable, and ready for the next stage of growth.
